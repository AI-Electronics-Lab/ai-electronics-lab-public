# Product

Status: draft. Not approved for public publication yet.

## What AI Electronics Lab is

AI Electronics Lab is a public-facing product and community space being prepared around AI-assisted electronics workflows.

The intended direction is to help users:

- describe an electronics idea or circuit workflow;
- receive structured explanations and artifacts;
- inspect generated simulation-oriented outputs;
- understand limitations and caveats;
- iterate on questions and examples.

## What AI Electronics Lab is not

AI Electronics Lab is not a replacement for engineering judgment, lab measurement, safety review, compliance work, or hardware validation.

A simulation result or generated artifact should be treated as evidence about a model or workflow, not as proof that a physical circuit is correct, safe, manufacturable, or production-ready.

## Intended audience

Draft target audiences:

- electronics learners;
- hobbyists and makers;
- engineers exploring early ideas;
- educators preparing examples;
- AI/electronics workflow researchers.
