# FAQ

Status: draft. Not approved for public publication yet.

## What is AI Electronics Lab?

AI Electronics Lab is being prepared as a public product and community space for AI-assisted electronics workflows, explanations, and simulation-oriented artifacts.

## Can it prove that my hardware will work?

No. A generated result, simulation, or explanation is not physical proof. Hardware work still requires engineering judgment, review, measurement, and safety practices.

## Is this repository public yet?

No. This repository is private-first while content is being drafted and reviewed.

## Who reviews technical claims?

Claims about electronics correctness, SPICE behavior, schematic validity, calculation accuracy, units, tolerances, safety, or hardware validity require Technical review before publication.

## Who approves publication?

Human approval is required before publication or repository visibility changes.

## What should not be added here?

Do not add secrets, credentials, internal hostnames, private runbook details, private artifact paths, or unapproved internal screenshots/evidence.
